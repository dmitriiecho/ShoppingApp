ShoppingApp deep links

Tap a link: Safari asks whether to open the app, and the app should open on the right screen. The links use the app's own scheme, shoppingapp://.

Open the app

Catalog shoppingapp://catalog
The Catalog tab, the product list.
Cart shoppingapp://cart
The Cart tab.
Product 1 shoppingapp://product/1
Product details in the Catalog tab. Back returns to the product list.

Trailing slash

Catalog shoppingapp://catalog/
Same as shoppingapp://catalog.
Cart shoppingapp://cart/
Same as shoppingapp://cart.
Product 1 shoppingapp://product/1/
Same as shoppingapp://product/1.

Edge cases

Missing product shoppingapp://product/999
Product details with the message “Product not found”.
Unknown address shoppingapp://catalog/shoes
The app opens on the screen it was on.
